# Кузнечный молот 16×16

Создан встроенным image_gen. Исходный результат уменьшен до 16×16 методом ближайшего соседа с сохранением альфа-канала. `hammer-16.png` — итоговая игровая текстура; увеличенное изображение служит только предпросмотром.

## Prompt

Create one Minecraft vanilla blacksmith hammer item sprite. STRICT 16 by 16 logical pixel grid, enlarged by nearest-neighbor to fill a square image. Every logical pixel must be a large solid square, exactly 1/16 of canvas width/height, grid perfectly aligned to canvas edges. This is extremely low resolution programmer pixel art, NOT high quality illustration. Maximum 7 flat colors: 4 iron gray shades, 3 brown wooden shades. Transparent background with actual alpha, no checkerboard, no ground/shadow. Hammer diagonal, wooden handle extends from bottom left toward upper right, short chunky rectangular iron hammer head across the upper right, perpendicular to handle. Simple blunt hammer head, no spike, no claw, no axe blade. Vanilla Minecraft item style. Entire icon fits within inner 14 by 14 cells with transparent one-cell border on all sides. No ornament, no straps, no tiny details, no antialiasing, no gradients, no text. Think iron axe vanilla 16px palette and pixel density, but a blacksmith hammer silhouette. Output ONLY this single sprite.
